How to add this to Outlook (once)
  1. Copy this link: https://vmwhite.github.io/tools/seminars.ics
  2. In Outlook (web or desktop), go to Calendar → Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
  3. Paste the link, give it a name (e.g. "Seminar Calendar"), and save.
  4. Outlook will check the link for updates periodically on its own — no need to redo this.

Tell your students to do the same with that one link, and their calendars will stay in sync automatically.
